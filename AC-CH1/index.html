Chapter 1: The New W`rld

Part 1: The begining

The landing-pod slammed into the loamy soil of Agro-c like a meteor. Steam hissed from the scorched hull as the hatch blew open, spitting out three figures into a sea of swaying golden stalks that stretched to the horizon. The air smelled like wet earth, crushed chlorophyll, and something faintly metallic. Blood, maybe, or just old machinery rusting under the sun.

Fai stepped out first, her labcoat flapping over her battered spacesuit like a mad scientist's cape. Short black hair with jagged yellow streaks whipped across her face, black eyes gleaming with that particular brand of unhinged curiosity that made people back away slowly. She was [redacted] technically, but that didn't cover the manic energy that rolled off her.

FAI
"So this is the new planet, huh?"

She said, voice bright and cracking at the edges. She spread her arms wide, taking in the endless fields, the distant silhouettes of abandoned agro-domes, and the thick, overgrown forests that looked like they'd swallowed whole civilizations.

FAI
"Focus on the mission. Burn everything on your path. We must find what we came here for."

A manic little laugh bubbled out of her, the kind that started low in the throat and ended somewhere near a hyena's cackle.

Behind her, Lex climbed down more carefully. A shy boy drone was compact, soft blue optics lines that flickered nervously. He clutched a scanner like it was his blanket.

LEX
"I don't think that's... ethical Captain," he muttered, voice soft and glitching with hesitation.
LEX
"These fields look like they're still semi-automated. Could the system be still working...?"

Fai spun on her heel, grinning with too many teeth for a human.

FAI
"You call it ethics, I call it boriiiiiiing." She drew the word out like taffy, eyes sparkling.
FAI
"What do you think, Vex?"

Vex didn't answer with words. She never did. The depressed girl drone dropped from the pod with a heavy metallic thud, her longer limbs and cracked purple visor giving her a permanent "done with existence" slouch. She raised one hand in a lazy thumbs-up, then immediately went back to staring at the dirt.

FAI
"See? Vex gets it. Burn protocol engaged."
LEX
*Sighs, the sound coming out as a static-heavy huff.* "We're tracking a distress signal, not declaring war on agriculture... Captain."

I don't know how long it's been...

The signal. That was the hook buried deep in Fai's ribs. It had pinged their ship two cycles ago. Her old human encryption, fragmented, but the voice signature... she knew it. She knew it the way you know the shape of a scar. Her old mentor. The man who'd taught her how to dissect, how to laugh while the universe tried to eat you. Father figure in all but name. Then he'd vanished into the black, chasing ghosts.

Now the ghost was screaming for help from a planet that looked like Earth after it decided to go full feral farm.

The three of them moved through the stalks. Fai's boots crushed glowing blue bio-robotic insects that popped like tiny fireworks. Lex kept the scanner raised, feeding data into his wrist display. Vex trailed behind, occasionally kicking over a rusted harvesting drone half-buried in the dirt. Its broken optic flickered once as she passed—then died again.

LEX
"Signal's strongest toward that ridge. But the readings are weird. Some interference. Almost like... the planet itself is answering back?"
FAI
"Good. I love it when the patient talks. Makes the dissection more interesting."

A low wind moved through the fields, carrying a sound that might have been rustling leaves or might have been something large dragging itself through the undergrowth.

Vex paused, head tilting. She made a single, slow gesture with two fingers, then gave another thumbs-up, this one slower. Almost sarcastic.

Fai laughed again, but there was an edge to it now. The kind of laugh that remembered every creepypasta she'd grown up on, every story where the rescue mission turned out to be the bait.

FAI
"Keep your optics peeled, team."

Pulling a cutter from her coat that definitely wasn't standard issue. Her helmet caught the sunlight like warning lights.

FAI
"Whatever sent that signal... it's been waiting for too long. And I don't plan on keeping it bored."

Behind them, the pod's emergency beacon blinked once, twice, then went dark as something in the tall stalks leaned in to listen.

A whole new world to explore

And They were hungry for this adventure...

...

The scanner in Lex's hands flatlined with a bizzing sound. The distress signal cut off mid-static like someone had yanked the plug.

Fai froze mid-stride, her eyes widening.

FAI
"What the...? No, no, no! Come on you piece of..."

A colorful string of swears exploded out of her, the kind that would've gotten her kicked out of any respectable lab back on the ship. She kicked a clump of glowing fungi so hard it burst into sparkling spores.

LEX
"Language, Captain."

Lex said automatically, voice soft but scandalized, blue optics flickering.

FAI
"You're not my dad."

Lex opened his mouth, then closed it. He turned to Vex for backup.

LEX
"Vex, back me up here?"

Vex didn't even look at him. She just slowly raised one metallic hand and gave him a very deliberate middle finger.

LEX
"Not you too..."

Lex groaned, shoulders slumping like his servos had given up on life.

FAI
"That's my girl."

Fai barked a sharp laugh and ruffled the top of Vex's head.

The momentary chaos died fast.

Because that's when the voices started.

Not words exactly. Wet, rustling murmurs coming from the thick bushes to their left—half-muffled giggles mixed with something that sounded like chewing.

The squad had zero weapons beyond Fai's cutter and whatever sarcastic attitude Vex could weaponize. They immediately dropped into the most awkward defensive formation in recorded history: Fai waving her cutter like a lightsaber, Lex hiding halfway behind her while holding the scanner like a shield, and Vex just standing there with her arms crossed, visor half-lidded in existential defeat.

The bushes parted.

Out stumbled a drone.

She was short, even compared to Lex, with messy pink hair matted down by years of neglect. Her white sweater was stained green with moss and sprouting tiny fungal caps like living jewelry. Empty black visor at first, body groaning with every stiff step like a zombie. She dragged one foot, leaving a trail of dirt and petals.

Then she stopped. A long yawn escaped her and her visor flickered to life with two bright yellow optics, soft and round.

STRAWBERRY
"[Giggles] Berry nice to meet you, travelers."

She said, voice syrupy sweet with a crackle of old audio damage. She shrugged hard, sending moss and mushrooms flying off her chassis in clumps.

STRAWBERRY
"[Gasp] What a surprise! Haven't had visitors in... oof, a really long time."
FAI
"Uh... hi?"

The trio introduced themselves quickly: Fai (still twitchy), Lex (polite nod), and Vex (single thumbs-down for hello).

STRAWBERRY
"[Happy expression] A real human?! In the flesh?! You're so... squishy and warm-looking! I thought you guys were all gone or turned into skeleton decorations!"

Fai's manic energy dimmed for half a second.

FAI
"Speaking of... have you seen another human around here? Older guy. Probably yelling into a radio."

Strawberry blinked. She turned and pointed a little ways into the field. There, half-swallowed by vines and blooming flowers, was a human skeleton picked clean and wearing the tattered remains of a lab coat very similar to Fai's. A single yellow stripe still clung to a patch of fabric.

Fai stared. Her jaw tightened, but she didn't scream or cry. Not yet. That wasn't the signal. That skeleton had been there for ages.

STRAWBERRY
"I don't know about any signals, [Giggles] but the village might! Lots of old stuff there. C'mon, I'll show—"

A roar tore through the fields.

It wasn't animal. It wasn't machine...

It was something alien and wrong, like a thousand throats screaming through soil and roots. The stalks around them shivered as if the planet itself flinched.

Strawberry's happy expression shattered. Her yellow optics flickered with raw panic.

STRAWBERRY
"[Gasp] Maksur! Oh no no no—we have to go! Really, really fast!"

She grabbed Fai's sleeve with surprising strength and started dragging her toward the distant tree line. Lex scrambled after them, scanner clutched tight.

Vex sighed the longest, most exhausted sigh her vocal processor could manage, then jogged to keep up.

Behind them, something massive moved through the golden fields—stalks flattening, wet crunching sounds following. Whatever a Maksur was, it was coming.

And it sounded hungry...
And it sounded hungry...

Strawberry didn't let go of Fai's sleeve. Her grip was cold metal wrapped in something too soft for comfort, like a child's hand fused with rusted rebar. She tugged them forward at a pace that was half skip, half panicked scramble, pink hair bouncing like it had a mind of its own.

The bright fields gave way to twisted undergrowth where the plants looked off.

STRAWBERRY
"[Giggles] Outlands are super infested with Maksurs right now."

Strawberry sang, not even breathing hard.

STRAWBERRY
"They get extra bitey when the sun start to rise. Berry bad timing for visitors!"

Fai tried to dig her heels in, labcoat snagging on thorns that bled clear sap.

FAI
"Wait—hold up. Who even are you? What is this planet? What the hell are Maksurs? And why do you look like you've been rolling around in a scrapyard for a decade?"

Strawberry's yellow optics flicked sideways, soft and round and completely unreadable. She tilted her head with a little mechanical whirr.

STRAWBERRY
"[Broken]"
FAI
"...What?"
STRAWBERRY
"[Soft smile] Broken"

Strawberry repeated cheerfully, like that explained everything in the universe. She gave Fai's sleeve another insistent tug.

STRAWBERRY
"Now you! Tell me about you, squishy friend! Where's home? You look like you've got stories. I love stories."

Fai blinked, caught off guard by the sudden subject flip. Behind her, Lex nearly tripped over a root, scanner sparking uselessly. Vex just trudged along, visor half-dimmed in quiet suffering.

Fai exhaled sharply, yellow-streaked hair falling into her face as they pushed deeper into the thickening woods.

FAI
"Fine. Just… living in a small colony on an asteroid, really. JCJenson forgot about us years ago. Classic evil capitalism. Ship us out, drain the resources, then ghost the whole operation like we were trash. So we made our own civilization. Built habitats, labs, the works."

Her voice darkened, grin turning sharp.

FAI
"Then the cult stuff started. People getting… ideas. Sacrifices. Internal purges. We were killing each other over power cells and prayer protocols by the end. Then that signal came through. Lex?"

Lex fumbled with his wrist display, blue optics flickering in concentration.

LEX
"Sorry, boss. No signal yet. The interference is getting worse like the planet's chewing on the frequency."

He glanced nervously at Strawberry's back, then leaned closer to Fai.

LEX
"Boss… should we really trust her?"

Fai let out a sharp, manic bark of laughter that echoed a little too loudly through the red-tinged trees.

FAI
"Oh sure, Lex. Let's totally trust the helpful guide who conveniently showed up at the exact right time to be the horror final boss villain. Classic trope."

She rolled her eyes.

FAI
"Keep your guard up, team. We do not trust her."

Strawberry let out a light cough a small, delicate sound that didn't belong in a drone's vocal processor. Everyone froze for half a second. Drones didn't cough. Drones didn't need to cough. Lex's head snapped toward her so fast his neck servos whined. Vex's visor brightened with mild interest for the first time all day.

But Strawberry just waved one hand like it was nothing.

STRAWBERRY
"[Gasp] Oopsie~ We're getting close! Hurry hurry"

She giggled again, the sound glitching at the edges like corrupted audio, and picked up the pace.

The trees grew denser, their bark a deep, bruised red that seemed to drink the light. Strange glowing fruit hung from branches like lanterns made of meat. Fai's cutter stayed hot in her grip, humming with barely-contained energy. She kept glancing at Strawberry's back. Those tiny fungal caps sprouting from her sweater, the way her left leg dragged just a little too perfectly.

Something massive crashed through the undergrowth far behind them. Wet. Heavy. Followed by a chorus of low, gurgling roars that vibrated up through their boots.

STRAWBERRY
"[Happy expression] Almost there"

Strawberry chirped, yellow optics sparkling with something that might have been fear, excitement, or both.

STRAWBERRY
"Just a little further and you'll be safe. Berry safe. Promise!"

Fai's manic grin returned, but it didn't reach her eyes.

FAI
"Yeah… safe. Sure."

The red sector waited somewhere ahead, hidden in the blood-colored trees. Whatever waited there.

The village or a trap. It kept its secrets wrapped tight behind Strawberry's sweet, broken smile.

And the Maksurs were still coming.
